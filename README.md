## 📝 Project description

This repository contains scripts and resources developed for creating **Children in Wales: The Cohort for Health and wellbeing inequality (CWTCH) Cohort** and **Research-Ready Data Asset (RRDA)** within the Secure Anonymised Information Linkage (SAIL) Databank.

The RRDA:
- Contains a cohort of individuals who have a record of being born or living in Wales between 0 and 18 with a birth date on or after January 1st 2000 and their linked mothers. This cohort is separated into 3 sub-cohorts, each of which contain a range of varaibles relevent to that cohot of people:
    - Birth Cohort - All children with a birth record in SAIL 
    - Child Cohort - All children who have a recorded residence in Wales (appear in the Welsh Demographic Service Dataset - WDSD)
    - Mother Cohort - All mothers linked to a child from the Birth Cohort using birth records
- Additionally the RRDA holds a selection of derrived supplementary tables and cohort to help aid research:
    -  Residency supplmentary cohort - This is a cohort of all people who have ever lived with the child and the relevent details of that residency.
    -  Education tables - Educational data relating to the children can be found in two tables;
        - Outcome - Data relating the results of a childs education
        - Wellbeing -  Data relating to student wellebing - including school mobility, special educational needs and fre school meal eligibility
    - Looked After Children - Patient level information on a child's journey through being looked after
    - Admin - This table provides a usefule single location to source fixed information relating to the child and mother such a week of birth (WOB) and sex, in addition to details about their coverage and which datasets they appear in
